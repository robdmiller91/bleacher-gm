Prospects with an ESPN id (public/data/espn_ids.csv) show their ESPN college headshot, hotlinked from
a.espncdn.com. Nothing is downloaded or stored here.

A <id>.jpg in this folder is only used for a prospect with no ESPN id. Add one to espn_ids.csv
(Player,School,ESPN Id; the id is the number in the player's espn.com/college-football URL) to switch them over.
